# DoorGo Product Rules

## Product purpose
DoorGo is an internal door-shop operations application covering site measure, job intake, work orders, production, scheduling, documents, tools, and reports.

## Product behavior
- Internal users already know the business and help shape the app.
- Interface wording must be short, practical, and instructional—not promotional.
- Mobile layouts require large touch targets and must work on large Android phones and Apple devices.
- Light theme is the standard/default interface.
- Dark theme is optional and should retain content and image parity.
- Printing always uses a dedicated light print layout, regardless of selected theme.
- Browser-local theme preference is preferred; account preference is the fallback.

## Brand and home-page wording
`docs/DOORGO_BRAND_AND_UI.md` is the primary summary authority for approved branding, the mark, and concise home-page area wording. Keep home-page descriptions short, practical, and non-promotional; do not duplicate or expand that wording here.

## Workflow principles
- Prefer a simple main screen plus a focused workspace for complex tasks.
- Progressive disclosure is preferred over showing every field at once.
- Real shop outputs and production usability take priority over generic software conventions.
- A saved job is the authoritative input record; documents and calculations derive from it.
- Do not infer critical operational values from titles, printed layouts, or screenshots when structured data exists.
